What is PrivX Pay? ›

PrivX Pay is a self-custodial private cash wallet for PulseChain stablecoins (DAI, pSunDAI, USDC). It uses zero-knowledge proofs to break the on-chain link between your deposit and your spending.

Think of it like a digital equivalent of withdrawing cash from a bank: once withdrawn, spending leaves no trail back to your account.

The "bill" model ZK proofs are computed in advance during Restock — not at point of sale. Each bill is a fresh wallet holding stablecoins. Paying someone is a plain token transfer: instant, cheap, and no ZK computation at checkout.
This is a bearer instrument Bills work like physical cash. Whoever holds the private key can spend the bill. There is no reversal, no dispute, no recovery.
🚀
First-Time Setup ›
1
Add to Home Screen — in Safari tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". In Chrome tap the menu → "Install App". This gives you the full-screen experience and enables offline use.
2
Generate your wallet — tap "Generate My Wallet". A 12-word seed phrase is created. It controls your primary wallet address. Bills and notes are preserved separately via the encrypted backup in the Wallet tab.
3
Write down your seed phrase — on paper, in order, stored somewhere safe. Do not screenshot it. Do not store it digitally. This is the only way to recover your wallet.
4
Fund your primary wallet — go to the Receive tab, copy your address. Send PLS for gas (you need ~150 PLS per bill you plan to create, plus enough stablecoins to shield). For maximum privacy, fund from a previously shielded fresh wallet — not directly from an exchange. See "Best Privacy Practices" below.
5
Shield and create bills — see the Restock guide below. Do this on WiFi with the screen on; it takes a few minutes per bill.
No MetaMask needed PrivX Pay manages its own keys. You never connect an external wallet. Your seed phrase IS the wallet.
🛡
Restock: Shielding & Creating Bills ›
Step 1 — Shield tokens (Shield tab)
1
Go to Restock → Shield. Select your token (DAI / pSunDAI / USDC) and denomination ($1 to $100).
2
Tap Deposit. The app approves the token spend then deposits into the PrivX privacy pool contract. A 0.5% fee is added on top of the denomination and sent to the fee vault — the pool holds exactly the denomination, so your bill pays out the full amount.
3
A note is saved locally — it looks like hp-dai-100-abc…-def…. This note is your claim ticket. Keep your phone safe; the note only exists in this app.
One note = one bill Each note can only be withdrawn once. The ZK proof spends the nullifier on-chain permanently.
Step 2 — Create bills (Create Bills tab)
1
Go to Restock → Create Bills. Tap your note to select it, then tap Create Bill.
2
Tap Create Bill. The app fetches the Merkle tree, generates a ZK proof, then asks you to choose: Auto-Relay (a proxy wallet submits the withdrawal — fast, ~150 PLS) or Export Proof (download proof JSON for a separate relayer — max privacy, no PLS needed upfront). Keep the screen on — the ZK proof takes 2–5 minutes on mobile.
3
When done the bill appears in your Wallet tab, ready to spend.
WiFi recommended The 53 MB proving key is downloaded once from IPFS on first use and cached in the app. The ZK computation itself is CPU-intensive and takes 2–5 minutes on mobile. Do this at home, not while standing at a register.
Multiple denominations Create bills in several denominations so you can make exact change. E.g. shield a $100, then a $50, a $20, and several $5s and $1s so you can pay any amount exactly.
Cash Notes Once a bill is ready, expand it in your Wallet tab and tap 🖨 Print Note. A print-ready bearer note is generated — hand it to anyone and they scan the QR with PrivX Pay to instantly receive the funds. No phone needed on the sender's side at the moment of exchange.

Paying Someone ›
1
Go to the Pay tab. Enter the recipient's wallet address, or tap Scan to read their QR code. Scanning a PrivX Pay Cash Note automatically opens the redeem flow instead.
2
Enter the total amount. The app automatically selects bills to cover the exact total (largest first). The amount must be achievable exactly from your available bills — no change is made.
3
Review the confirmation screen — it shows which bills will be spent. Tap Send Payment.
4
Each bill wallet sends an ERC20 transfer to the recipient. Payment confirms within seconds on PulseChain.
No ZK at checkout Paying is just a token transfer — it confirms in one block (~3 seconds). The privacy was established days or weeks earlier when you created the bill.
Exact change required The app selects bills automatically (largest first) but cannot make change. If you owe $47 and your smallest combination using available bills doesn't hit $47 exactly, the payment will be blocked. Shield multiple denominations — a mix of $20, $10, $5, and $1 bills gives you the most flexibility.

Security Advice ›
Your seed phrase is everything Anyone who has your 12 words can import your wallet and sweep all bills and all funds. Never type it into any website, app, or chat. Never photograph it. Write it on paper, store it like cash.
Two things to back up Your seed phrase recovers your primary wallet. Your encrypted device backup (Wallet tab → Export Backup) recovers your bills, notes, and history. Without a backup, clearing app data or switching devices means permanent loss of all active bills. Keep both.
Bills are hot wallet funds Bill wallets are stored encrypted in your PIN-protected vault. Treat bills like cash in your pocket — keep only what you need for near-term spending. Large savings should stay in cold storage and be shielded in small batches.
Bill theft risk If someone obtains your bill's private key (e.g. device compromise), they can sweep that bill. The bill owner has no recourse — it's the same as losing a banknote.
Best practices · Keep your device locked with a strong PIN or biometrics
· Create bills from different deposit events for better anonymity
· Wait some time between shielding and creating bills
· Don't reuse the same bill wallet for multiple payments
· Don't discuss your balance or transactions in linked accounts
Privacy model PrivX Pay uses the PrivX Hurricane PLONK protocol. Your deposit is added to a 2^14-leaf Merkle tree. The ZK proof proves membership without revealing which leaf — anonymity set of up to 16,384 deposits per pool (larger set = stronger privacy, which is why waiting after deposit matters).

Auto-Relay: your primary wallet funds an ephemeral proxy, which calls the shield. You never call the shield directly, but the chain primary → proxy → shield is traceable on-chain by a determined analyst.
Export Proof: an independent third party submits the withdrawal from their own wallet. Your primary wallet has zero on-chain connection to the shield or the bill wallet. This is the maximum privacy path.
What PrivX Pay does NOT hide · The fact that you used PrivX Pay (on-chain deposit is public)
· The denomination and token of your deposit
· The final ERC20 transfer at point of sale (recipient, amount, token)
· Metadata: your IP, timing, device fingerprint (use a VPN + Tor browser for maximum privacy)

Step-by-step optimal setup
1
Get a "clean" PLS source first. Before you even open PrivX Pay, shield some PLS through PrivX Hurricane from your main wallet. Withdraw to a brand new address. This fresh wallet is your funding source — it has no link to your identity.
2
Get stablecoins into your PrivX Pay wallet. Two ways — both maintain privacy since your PLS source is already clean:

Option A: If your clean source wallet already holds stablecoins (DAI, USDC etc.) — send them directly to your PrivX Pay address. Simplest path.

Option B: Use the built-in Restock → Swap tab to swap PLS for stablecoins directly — no MetaMask, no external site. The swap is visible on-chain, but since the PLS has no identity link, privacy is maintained.
3
Shield stablecoins into PrivX Pay. Go to Restock → Shield. This deposits into the Hurricane PLONK pool. Your primary wallet is now the depositor — visible on-chain, but untraceable to your identity because of step 1–2.
4
Wait before creating bills. Let some time pass between your deposit and your ZK withdrawal. More deposits from other users enter the pool, increasing your anonymity set. Hours is good; days is better.
5
Create bills when you're ready to spend. After the ZK proof, choose Auto-Relay (your primary wallet funds a proxy which submits the withdrawal — fast, one-hop chain link) or Export Proof (hand the proof JSON to an independent relayer via the bundled Hurricane Proof Relayer — zero on-chain link, maximum privacy). Either way, the bill wallet appears with no history — it just suddenly holds stablecoins.
6
Pay from bill wallets. The recipient sees only: an ERC20 transfer from a random fresh wallet. No link to you, your primary wallet, or your exchange account.
Don't skip step 1 If you fund your PrivX Pay wallet directly from an exchange or KYC'd wallet, your primary wallet is linked to your identity on-chain. The shield + ZK still breaks the link to your bills, but blockchain analytics can see "exchange address → PrivX Pay primary wallet → shield deposit." Start clean.
Denomination linkage The denomination you deposit ($1, $10, $100 etc.) is visible on-chain. If you deposit a unique amount at a unique time, that alone can narrow the anonymity set. Stick to standard denominations and avoid depositing immediately before spending.
What the ZK proof actually hides The proof proves you know the secret behind one of the 16,384 leaves in the Merkle tree — without revealing which one. An observer sees a valid withdrawal from the pool but cannot determine which deposit it corresponds to. This is the core privacy guarantee.
Additional hardening · Use a VPN or Tor when accessing the app — your IP is not hidden by ZK proofs
· Don't correlate payment amounts with known purchases on linked accounts
· Create bills across multiple sessions, not all at once
· Never share a bill's private key digitally — the exception is printing a Cash Note for an immediate physical handoff
· The app works fully offline after first load — use it offline when possible

FAQ & Troubleshooting ›
Proof generation is taking forever / failed
The 53 MB proving key is downloaded once from IPFS on first use, then cached in the app — no repeat download needed. On older phones the ZK computation itself can take 10+ minutes. Keep the screen on. If the proof fails, try again — the cached key loads instantly on retry.
I can't make exact change
Restock more bills in smaller denominations ($1, $5, $10). The pay algorithm requires an exact match. Plan your denomination mix based on how you expect to spend.
My bill shows "funding gas…"
In Auto-Relay mode, each bill uses ~150 PLS from your primary wallet as a gas budget sent to the bill wallet. Check your PLS balance on the Receive tab. In Export Proof mode, tap "Check Relay" on the bill card after the relay confirms — gas is funded automatically.
I switched browsers / devices — my bills are gone
Bills are stored in your encrypted local vault. Your seed phrase re-derives your primary wallet address, but bill private keys are not embedded in the seed — they were generated fresh at creation. To recover bills on a new device, use the encrypted backup: Wallet tab → Export Backup before switching devices, then Import on the new one. The backup is encrypted with your seed phrase so only you can open it. Without a backup, bills from withdrawn notes are gone permanently. Treat bills like cash — don't keep more on-device than you need.
Can I send bills to another person?
Yes — through Cash Notes. Tap a ready bill in your Wallet tab, expand it, and tap 🖨 Print Note. This opens a print-ready bearer note with a QR code. Hand the printed note to anyone — they open PrivX Pay, go to the Pay tab, tap Scan, and point their camera at the QR. The funds transfer to their wallet automatically using the gas already in the bill. Treat a printed note exactly like physical cash: whoever scans the QR first receives the funds, and there is no reversal.
What is the 0.5% fee?
The PrivX protocol adds a 0.5% fee on top of the denomination at deposit. To create a $100 bill you approve $100.50 — $100 goes into the pool, $0.50 goes to the fee vault. The bill pays out exactly $100 on withdrawal. There is no fee on withdrawal or spending.
What is PRIVX and why do I earn it?
PRIVX is the native token of the PrivX protocol (contract: 0x34310B5d3a8d1e5f8e4A40dcf38E48d90170E986). Every successful ZK withdrawal earns a mining reward sent directly to your bill wallet by the shield contract. After you spend a bill, the reward card lets you claim PRIVX to any address you choose. For maximum privacy send it to a fresh wallet with no connection to this app — that way there is no on-chain link between the bill and your identity. The PRIVX stays in the bill wallet until you claim it, so you can take your time. Spent bills remain accessible in your Wallet tab if you need to claim later.
Is this open source / audited?
The PrivX Hurricane PLONK shield contracts are immutable and deployed on PulseChain — no upgrade keys, no admin controls. All contract addresses are listed in the Contract Addresses section and are publicly readable on PulseScan. The proving key is deterministic and its IPFS CID is published. This app is a fully client-side interface with no server — you can verify every network request it makes in your browser's developer tools.

Convert Protocol Fees ›

Every deposit pays a 0.5% fee in the shielded token. Fees accumulate in the Fee Vault until anyone triggers a conversion — they're swapped to PRIVX, then split: 80% locked as permanent PRIVX/WPLS liquidity, 10% added to the POP Mining Vault (funds withdrawal rewards), 10% burned permanently. This is a public community function — no special role or permission required. Anyone can call it, at any time.

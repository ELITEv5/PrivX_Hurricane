What is Hurricane Pay?
Hurricane Pay turns stablecoins into private digital cash — like a $20 bill, but on-chain. You deposit DAI, pSunDAI, or USDC and receive a private note. The note proves ownership of exactly that amount, with no on-chain connection to your wallet. Hand the note to a merchant, they redeem it — you've paid privately.

Accepting a Hurricane Pay note:
1. Paste the customer's note (or scan their QR and copy the text).
2. Enter your wallet address — this is where funds will arrive.
3. Connect a wallet to pay the gas fee (PLS), then click Redeem.
4. Your browser generates a ZK proof, then submits the withdrawal on-chain.
Proof generation takes 1–3 minutes on desktop and longer on mobile. Keep this tab open — do not refresh.
You receive the full denomination — the 0.5% fee was paid by the buyer on deposit. You also earn PRIVX mining rewards for every redemption.

Privacy & Security
How is my privacy protected?
When you deposit, a cryptographic commitment is stored on-chain — not your address or amount. When the note is redeemed, a zero-knowledge proof confirms the note is valid without revealing which deposit it came from. No on-chain link between sender and receiver exists.
What if I lose my note?
The note is your only key. If lost, the funds in that shield slot cannot be recovered by anyone — including the protocol. Back up notes immediately after buying, ideally in a password manager or encrypted storage.
Can a note be used twice?
No. Each note has a unique nullifier that is recorded on-chain the moment it's redeemed. Any second attempt with the same note will revert. This is enforced by the smart contract, not the UI.
What are PRIVX mining rewards?
Merchants who redeem notes earn PRIVX tokens from the Hurricane Pay Mining Vault. Rewards scale with denomination — larger bills earn more PRIVX per redemption. Rewards reduce proportionally as the vault empties and refill when the community tops it up.
Who controls the contracts?
The Hurricane Pay shields are fully immutable after deployment — no admin can pause, upgrade, or confiscate funds. The protocol fee (0.5%) and mining rewards are set at construction and cannot be changed.

What is a Cash Note?
A PrivX Pay Cash Note is a printed bearer instrument — private digital cash you can fold in your wallet and hand to anyone, no phone needed on either side of the exchange. Each note encodes a shielded stablecoin denomination. Whoever scans the QR first redeems the value — exactly like physical cash.
1 Deposit stablecoins on the PrivX Pay ATM — you receive a private note string (hp-dai-20-…)
2 Paste the note string below and click Print Note — a US dollar-sized bearer note is generated in your browser
3 Hand the printed note to anyone — they open PrivX Pay, go to the Redeem tab, and scan the QR to receive the funds on-chain
⚠ Bearer instrument — treat exactly like a physical banknote. Anyone with access to the QR code or note string can redeem it. Keep face-down until handoff. Once handed over, it cannot be cancelled.
Format: hp-{token}-{denom}-{nullifier}-{secret} · copy from PrivX Pay ATM or Wallet tab

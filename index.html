⚡ How It Works

1
Get what you need

You need pSunDAI (the stablecoin being shielded) and PRIVX (the protocol fee token). Both are on PulseChain. Connect MetaMask to PulseChain and make sure you have PLS for gas.

2
Choose a denomination and deposit

Pick 100, 1,000, 10,000, or 100,000 pSunDAI. You approve two amounts: the pSunDAI you're shielding, and the PRIVX protocol fee (80% is burned forever, 20% seeds the mining vault). Your deposit is recorded on-chain as a cryptographic commitment — not your address.

3
Save your private note — this is critical

A secret note is generated entirely in your browser. It never touches a server. This note is the only way to recover your funds. Write it down. Store it encrypted. The note contains your nullifier and secret — the two values the ZK proof is built from.

4
Wait (optional but recommended)

The longer you wait, the more other deposits join the anonymity set. When there are 100 deposits before you withdraw, an observer cannot determine which of those 100 was yours. Waiting hours or days dramatically strengthens your privacy.

5
Withdraw to a fresh address

From a wallet that has never interacted with your deposit address, paste your note and choose a recipient. The app generates a PLONK zero-knowledge proof in your browser — proving you own a valid note without revealing which deposit it came from. The contract verifies the proof on-chain and releases your pSunDAI.

6
Receive pSunDAI + PRIVX mining reward

Your pSunDAI arrives at the recipient address. Simultaneously, the mining vault pays a PRIVX reward — a percentage of the original fee, depending on vault fullness. Early users earn more. The reward goes to the recipient address automatically.

🔥 The PRIVX Token

PRIVX is the protocol token of the PrivX Hurricane Shield. It is not a governance token, not a utility token, and not a security. It is a Proof-of-Privacy mining token — earned by using the protocol and deflationary by design.

🔥

80% of every deposit fee is burned forever. PRIVX supply decreases with every shield deposit. 21 million total supply, no minting.

⛏️

Mine PRIVX by withdrawing. Every successful withdrawal triggers a PRIVX reward from the mining vault — proportional to the vault's fullness. Early miners earn the most.

📉

Emission decays as the vault drains. The reward rate drops quadratically as PRIVX is paid out. Refilling the vault resets the curve upward.

🏛️

No dev wallet. The development team holds PRIVX and benefits from the burn mechanism — not from a fee stream. There is no admin key, no upgradeable proxy, no multisig.

🛡️ Why Financial Privacy Matters

Privacy is a fundamental human right

Article 12 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights protects individuals from arbitrary interference with their privacy. Financial privacy is inseparable from that right. When every transaction is visible to every employer, government, creditor, and advertiser — permanently, immutably, forever — the result is not transparency. It is total surveillance.

Blockchain without privacy is a panopticon

Public blockchains are revolutionary. They eliminate intermediaries, enable trustless contracts, and give individuals direct control over their assets. But complete transparency creates a new problem: a permanent, searchable ledger of everything you have ever bought, sold, earned, or donated. Employers screen wallets. Governments trace flows. Criminals target high balances. Transparency, without the option of privacy, is a surveillance system, not a financial system.

Privacy is not crime

Cash has been private for thousands of years. When you pay for groceries with a $20 bill, no one records your identity, the time, or your balance. That privacy is not suspicious — it is normal. Using a privacy shield to send stablecoins is the digital equivalent of using cash. The existence of a privacy tool does not imply wrongdoing any more than owning curtains implies criminality.

The separation of money and state

Financial surveillance is one of the most powerful tools of authoritarian control. Freezing accounts, debanking political opponents, and monitoring donations are not hypothetical risks — they are documented realities across dozens of countries. A privacy layer that is ownerless, immutable, and controlled by mathematics rather than institutions is a practical form of financial self-sovereignty. No entity can freeze it. No government can deplatform it. No company can be acquired and its policies reversed. It simply runs.

Why PrivX Hurricane specifically?

PrivX Hurricane is designed to be the most credibly neutral privacy protocol possible. There is no owner. No admin key. No upgradeability. The contracts are immutable from the moment of deployment. The development team cannot change the fee, pause withdrawals, or censor any transaction. The only rule is the circuit: if you know the note, you can withdraw — always, unconditionally, forever.

❓ FAQ

Is my note stored anywhere?

No. Your note is generated entirely inside your browser using the Web Crypto API. It is never sent to a server, never stored on-chain, and never leaves your device unless you copy it. If you lose it, there is no recovery mechanism — the funds are locked in the contract forever.

What is a zero-knowledge proof?

A ZK proof lets you prove you know a secret without revealing the secret itself. In this protocol, you prove "I know the nullifier and secret for a valid deposit in this Merkle tree" without revealing which deposit is yours. The on-chain verifier checks the mathematical proof — it cannot determine your identity, only that the proof is valid.

Why does denomination matter for privacy?

All deposits in a shield are the same size (e.g. exactly 100 pSunDAI). When you withdraw, the observer sees someone withdrew 100 pSunDAI but cannot distinguish which of the N depositors owns the note. Larger denominations have smaller anonymity sets — fewer people deposit 100,000 pSunDAI than 100 pSunDAI — so the 100 and 1,000 tiers generally offer stronger privacy.

Can the contracts be paused or upgraded?

No. There is no owner, no admin key, no proxy pattern, and no pause function. Once deployed, the contracts are mathematically immutable. Not the development team, not a multisig, not a DAO — no one can alter them.

What happens if the mining vault runs out of PRIVX?

Withdrawals still work normally — the vault reward is wrapped in a try/catch and never blocks a withdrawal. If the vault is empty, the reward is simply skipped. Anyone can refill the vault by calling topUp() with PRIVX, which resets the emission curve.

Why do I need PRIVX to deposit?

PRIVX is the protocol fee — it replaces the traditional model where a developer takes a cut of each transaction. Instead, 80% of your fee is burned (reducing supply permanently) and 20% funds the mining rewards. The developers hold PRIVX and benefit as the protocol grows — never from a direct fee stream.

Why does proof generation take so long?

PLONK zero-knowledge proofs require heavy computation — specifically, downloading the 29MB proving key and running polynomial operations on thousands of constraints. This happens entirely in your browser. Modern laptops take 15–30 seconds. This is a technical property of the cryptography and cannot be reduced without changing the security model.

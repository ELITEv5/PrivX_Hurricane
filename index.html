How It Works

1
Get PRIVX and connect your wallet

PrivX Hurricane currently shields PRIVX — the protocol's own privacy mining token. pSunDAI and other approved tokens are coming soon. You need PRIVX to deposit, and a small amount of PLS for gas. Connect MetaMask to PulseChain (chainId 369).

2
Choose a denomination and deposit

Pick 100, 1,000, 10,000, or 100,000 PRIVX. A 0.5% fee is deducted from your deposit in PRIVX and sent to the Fee Vault, which converts it into Protocol-Owned Liquidity and mining rewards. Your deposit is recorded on-chain as a cryptographic commitment — not your address.

3
Save your private note — this is critical

A secret note is generated entirely in your browser. It never touches a server. This note is the only way to recover your funds. Write it down. Store it encrypted. The note encodes your nullifier and secret — the two values the ZK proof is built from.

4
Wait (optional but recommended)

The longer you wait, the more other deposits join the anonymity set. When there are 100 deposits before you withdraw, an observer cannot determine which of those 100 was yours. Waiting hours or days dramatically strengthens your privacy.

5
Withdraw to a fresh address

From a wallet that has never interacted with your deposit address, paste your note and choose a recipient. The app generates a PLONK zero-knowledge proof in your browser — proving you own a valid note without revealing which deposit it came from. The contract verifies the proof on-chain and releases your tokens.

6
Receive your tokens + PRIVX mining reward

Your shielded tokens arrive at the recipient address. Simultaneously, the mining vault pays a PRIVX reward — a percentage of the original fee, depending on vault fullness. Early users earn more. The reward goes to the recipient address automatically.

The PRIVX Token POP

PRIVX is the protocol token of PrivX Hurricane. It is not a governance token, not a utility token, and not a security. It is a Proof-of-Privacy mining token — earned by using the protocol and deflationary by design.

Every deposit fee strengthens PRIVX. A 0.5% fee is collected in the shielded token and sent to the Fee Vault. The vault converts it into PRIVX/WPLS Protocol-Owned Liquidity (80%), Mining Vault top-up (10%), and permanent burn (10%). Fixed supply of 21 million — no minting ever.

Mine PRIVX by generating a Proof of Privacy (POP). Every successful withdrawal produces a POP — a zero-knowledge proof that you own a valid shielded note — and automatically triggers a PRIVX reward from the mining vault. No claim transaction needed. Early miners earn the most.

Rewards decay as the vault drains. The reward rate drops quadratically as PRIVX is paid out. The Fee Vault automatically refills the mining vault from every deposit across every shield — POP rewards are self-sustaining.

No dev fee stream. The development team holds PRIVX and benefits as the protocol grows through the POL and burn mechanism — never from a direct cut of transactions. There is no admin key, no upgradeable proxy, no multisig.

Public blockchains are revolutionary. They eliminate intermediaries, enable trustless contracts, and give individuals direct control over their assets. But complete transparency creates a new problem: a permanent, searchable ledger of everything you have ever bought, sold, earned, or donated. Employers screen wallets. Governments trace flows. Criminals target high balances. Transparency, without the option of privacy, is a surveillance system, not a financial system.

Cash has been private for thousands of years. When you pay for groceries with a $20 bill, no one records your identity, the time, or your balance. That privacy is not suspicious — it is normal. Using a privacy shield to send a Stable Asset is the digital equivalent of using cash. The existence of a privacy tool does not imply wrongdoing any more than owning curtains implies criminality.

FAQ

Is my note stored anywhere?

No. Your note is generated entirely inside your browser using the Web Crypto API. It is never sent to a server, never stored on-chain, and never leaves your device unless you copy it. If you lose it, there is no recovery mechanism — the funds are locked in the contract forever.

What is a zero-knowledge proof?

A ZK proof lets you prove you know a secret without revealing the secret itself. In this protocol, you prove "I know the nullifier and secret for a valid deposit in this Merkle tree" without revealing which deposit is yours. The on-chain verifier checks the mathematical proof — it cannot determine your identity, only that the proof is valid.

Why does denomination matter for privacy?

All deposits in a shield are the same size (e.g. exactly 100 of the shielded token). When you withdraw, the observer sees someone withdrew 100 units but cannot distinguish which of the N depositors owns the note. Larger denominations have smaller anonymity sets — so the 100 and 1,000 tiers generally offer stronger privacy.

Can the contracts be paused or upgraded?

No. There is no owner, no admin key, no proxy pattern, and no pause function. Once deployed, the contracts are mathematically immutable. Not the development team, not a multisig, not a DAO — no one can alter them.

What happens if the mining vault runs out of PRIVX?

Withdrawals still work normally — the vault reward is wrapped in a try/catch and never blocks a withdrawal. If the vault is empty, the reward is simply skipped. Anyone can refill the vault by calling topUp() with PRIVX, which resets the emission curve.

What is the deposit fee?

A 0.5% fee is taken from your deposit in the shielded token — no separate token required. For PRIVX shields, 0.5% of your PRIVX deposit goes to the Fee Vault. The vault converts it into Protocol-Owned Liquidity (deepening PRIVX/WPLS liquidity permanently), mining vault top-up (self-funding POP rewards), and a burn. The more shields are used, the stronger PRIVX becomes.

Why does proof generation take so long?

PLONK zero-knowledge proofs require heavy computation — specifically, downloading the 29MB proving key and running polynomial operations on thousands of constraints. This happens entirely in your browser. Modern laptops take 15–30 seconds. This is a fundamental property of the cryptography and cannot be reduced without changing the security model.

Will other tokens be supported?

Yes. pSunDAI and other approved tokens are coming soon. Every shield uses the same PLONK circuit, the same zero-knowledge proof, and the same fee model — only the token and denomination change. Any ERC-20 token can be shielded without modifying the circuit or verifier.

What Is PrivX Hurricane?

PrivX Hurricane is a zero-knowledge privacy protocol built on PulseChain. It lets you deposit tokens into a shielded pool and withdraw them to a completely unlinked address — with no on-chain connection between sender and recipient.

The withdrawal proof is a PLONK zero-knowledge proof generated entirely in your browser. It proves you know a valid deposit note without revealing which deposit it came from. The on-chain verifier checks the math. The contract releases your tokens. No one — not the development team, not a node operator, not a block explorer — can link your deposit to your withdrawal.

Every withdrawal also produces a Proof of Privacy (POP) — a verifiable record that you used the protocol — which triggers an automatic PRIVX mining reward. No claim transaction. No staking. Just use the protocol, earn PRIVX.

Architecture
Shield Contract (PrivX_Shield.sol)

One contract per token per denomination. Fully immutable after deployment — no owner, no admin key, no upgrade proxy, no pause function.

  • Merkle tree: 14-level incremental Poseidon Merkle tree (16,384 leaves)
  • Root history: Last 100 roots stored, allowing ~100 concurrent pending withdrawals
  • Circuit: PrivXMixer(14) — PLONK proof with public signals [root, nullifierHash, denomination]
  • Fee model: 0.5% of denomination in the shielded token, sent to FeeVault on deposit
  • POP rewards: mineReward() called on Mining Vault on every successful withdrawal
Fee Vault (PrivX_FeeVault.sol)

Receives protocol fees from all shields and converts them into PRIVX value. Anyone can trigger conversion at any time.

Mining Vault (PrivX_Mining_Vault_V2.sol)

Pays PRIVX rewards to withdrawal recipients. Sealed after shield registration.

  • Emission curve: Quadratic decay relative to peak balance — rewards are highest when vault is full
  • Auto-refill: Fee Vault calls topUp() on every conversion cycle
  • Cooldown: 5-minute per-user cooldown prevents reward spam
  • Max rate: 10% of the fee amount per withdrawal (hard cap)
ZK Circuit (circuits/PrivXMixer.circom)
Private inputs:  nullifier, secret, pathIndices[14], siblings[14]
Public signals:  root, nullifierHash, denomination

Constraints:
  commitment    = Poseidon(nullifier, secret)
  commitment    ∈ Merkle tree with root
  nullifierHash = Poseidon(nullifier, denomination)

The circuit binds nullifierHash to the denomination, so a note from one shield cannot be replayed in a different denomination pool. The same circuit and proving key power every shield — adding a new token requires no circuit changes.

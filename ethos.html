Fee Flow

0.5% fee on every deposit in the shielded token → Fee Vault → swapped to PRIVX → 80% Protocol-Owned Liquidity / 10% Mining Vault (POP rewards) / 10% permanent burn. No PRIVX required to use any shield.

ZK Circuit

Shared PrivXMixer(14) PLONK circuit — 14-level Poseidon Merkle tree, 16,384 leaves per pool. Same verifier and hasher as PrivX Hurricane. Proof generation in browser ~15–30s.
